How the flags work

  • First 48 hours: a small dip is normal. Losing more than 10% of birth weight is flagged red.
  • After day 2: each puppy should gain about 5–10% of its body weight per day. Under 5% is yellow. Any loss is red.
  • Day 10 on: a puppy that hasn't doubled its birth weight is flagged yellow at least.
  • The Lightest tag marks the smallest puppy at the latest weigh-in. That's usually the one to rotate onto a back teat or supplement with milk replacer.
  • Photos: tap + Photo on a puppy. A weekly photo becomes its profile picture, labeled by week of life. Marking photos are kept separately.
  • Weigh at the same time each day, before a feeding if you can. These flags are a guide, not a diagnosis. Call your vet about any red flag, or a puppy that is cold, weak, or crying nonstop.
